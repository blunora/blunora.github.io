Trend Radar · planned provider connection

Market research should arrive with its evidence.

Hermes includes an in-app Trend Radar research interface. The planned EverBee Research API integration is pending EverBee developer approval. Until access is approved and configured, Hermes does not send live requests to EverBee or show invented market data.

Subject to EverBee’s approval, scopes, and documented endpoints, the intended read-only research areas include Etsy keyword research, competitor research, listing analysis, and market trends. What Hermes can display will depend on the data the approved API actually provides.

Built with clear boundaries

Research informs decisions. It does not publish for you.

Server-side credentials

Provider credentials belong on the authenticated Hermes backend, never in the iOS app.

Read-only research intent

EverBee research is intended for analysis only, subject to the authorization EverBee grants.

Usage and budget controls

Research usage is designed to be tracked and bounded before a paid provider is activated.

No automatic listing changes

Research results do not automatically edit, create, or publish Etsy listings.
